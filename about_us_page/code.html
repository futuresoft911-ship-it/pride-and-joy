Our Story

Pride & Joy was born from a simple idea: to create a space where every member of the LGBTQ+ community can find apparel that truly represents them. It all started with a single T-shirt design and a passion for celebrating the beautiful diversity that makes us who we are. We believe that what you wear is a form of expression, a way to tell your story without saying a word.

From a small corner in our apartment to a brand that ships worldwide, our core mission has never changed. We're a small, queer-owned business dedicated to crafting high-quality, ethically-made apparel that you can wear with pride.

Our Mission & Values

Our mission is to empower the LGBTQ+ community through wearable art that sparks conversation, fosters connection, and celebrates every unique identity. We are committed to authenticity, inclusivity, and quality in everything we do.
